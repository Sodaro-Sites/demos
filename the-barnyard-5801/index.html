About The Barnyard

The Barnyard

The Barnyard is a Caribbean and soul food restaurant in Farmingdale, NY that blends traditional Caribbean cuisine with modern culinary techniques to create an unforgettable dining experience.

Our menu celebrates West Indian and Southern roots with specials like braised oxtails and flavorful jerk chicken, complemented by a variety of refreshing cocktails.

We offer brunch with a mix of classic and innovative dishes in a warm and inviting atmosphere where every visitor feels like family.
