About

Traditional Caribbean cooking, a modern kitchen

The Barnyard is a Caribbean and soul food restaurant in Farmingdale that blends traditional Caribbean cuisine with modern cooking techniques.

The menu celebrates West Indian and Southern roots, with specials like braised oxtails and jerk chicken and a variety of refreshing cocktails. Brunch brings classic and new dishes together.

A warm room where every visitor feels like family.
