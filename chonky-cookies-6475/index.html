Our story

From a home kitchen to a cult following

Heather started baking oversized, stuffed cookies when the pandemic began. They took off on Instagram, and in August 2021 she opened the first Chonky Cookies shop in Lindenhurst. Today the cookies are ordered online.

Made big on purpose. Thick, oversized cookies with something stuffed inside.

Follow the batches. New flavors show up first on Instagram, @chonky_cookies.
