What Spycoast is

Not just a bar. A family spot.

Owned by Pat Haufe, Sean Chamberlain and Mitch LaBianca, Spycoast brought its Port Jefferson pub to Patchogue's Main Street as a place for everyone: date night, the game, or Sunday lunch with the kids.

Small-batch cocktails

House signatures like the Pink Panther and the Purple Prince, plus draft beer and wine.

A full kitchen

Short ribs, salmon, pork chops, burgers, tacos and pizza, not just bar snacks.

Room for your party

Birthdays, showers, graduations, holiday parties and fundraisers.
